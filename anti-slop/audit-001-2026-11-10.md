## One-line reasons for the major choices (R-31)

Color: GitHub's own five greens on #0d1117 because the page's job is to look like your profile's graph. Layout: stacked single column because the calendar is wide and the panels are the content. Typography: Inter for reading, JetBrains Mono for data/terminal voice. Cards: one repeated panel shell; the six canvases are siblings of one idea, variation lives inside the canvas. Radius: 16px cards, pill only for chips. Spacing: tight around the graph, generous hero. Empty cells #21262d, not #ebedf0, because the theme is GitHub dark. Identity motif: the contribution calendar itself, re-drawn six ways.
